EasyDRF - SSTV Net installer
===========================

This installer sets up EasyDRF, a free HamDRM digital image program for
sending and receiving pictures over amateur radio.

EasyDRF was written by Daz Man, based on WinDRM by HB9TLK and the Dream
DRM software. This package installs Daz's official EasyDRF.exe, unchanged.
It is NOT an official EasyDRF release: the only additions are this
installer and preset settings for our SSTV net.

What the installer does:
  - Installs EasyDRF for your Windows user account (no admin needed)
  - Asks for your callsign and loads the net's standard settings
  - Adds Start menu and desktop shortcuts
  - Adds an "EasyDRF Received Images" shortcut to your received pictures

After installing, open EasyDRF and pick your radio's sound card under the
Soundcard menu (receive input and transmit output), and your PTT port under
Setup. The net's setup guide shows the settings for each radio.

Uninstalling removes the program but keeps your received pictures and your
settings.
